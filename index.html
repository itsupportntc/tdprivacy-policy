Privacy Policy

Effective Date:18 March 2026

This application is developed and maintained for healthcare service delivery under the Department of Health, Medical & Family Welfare, Government of Telangana. It is intended for use only by authorized personnel.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Patient Name
  • Mobile Number
  • Address
  • Age and Gender

Government Identification Data

  • Aadhaar Number (used strictly for patient identification)

Sensitive Health Information

  • Clinical Diagnosis
  • Laboratory Test Details
  • Investigation Data
  • Sample Collection Information
  • Barcode and Tracking Data

User Account Information

  • Username and Login Credentials
  • Facility / Organization Details

Device & Technical Data

  • Device Type and OS Version
  • App Usage Logs
  • Network and Sync Information

2. How We Use Information

The collected data is used for:

  • User authentication and secure login
  • Patient registration and identification
  • Laboratory and diagnostic workflow management
  • Barcode scanning and sample tracking
  • Data synchronization with government systems
  • Audit, reporting, and compliance

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data.

Data may be shared only with:

  • Authorized government healthcare systems
  • Approved hospitals and laboratories
  • Legal authorities when required by law

4. Data Retention

Data is retained as per government healthcare regulations and policies.

  • Stored for patient care and diagnostic purposes
  • Retained for audit, compliance, and reporting
  • Retention duration depends on regulatory requirements

5. Data Deletion

Users cannot directly delete patient data from the application.

To request deletion or correction:

  • Email: [YOUR OFFICIAL EMAIL]
  • Requests will be processed as per government policy

6. Permissions Used

  • Camera: For barcode scanning
  • Internet: For data synchronization
  • Storage: Temporary data handling

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures:

  • Secure login authentication
  • Encrypted communication (HTTPS)
  • Role-based access control

8. Children's Privacy

This application is not intended for use by children or the general public. It is strictly for authorized healthcare staff.
